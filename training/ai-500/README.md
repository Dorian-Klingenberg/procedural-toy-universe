# AI-500 study track

This directory organizes preparation for [Exam AI-500: Designing and Implementing Multi-Agent AI Solutions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-500).

## Start here

- [AI-500 readiness checklist](READINESS_CHECKLIST.md) — a starter checklist for objective mapping, multi-agent implementation evidence, and learning gaps.
- [Test me later](TEST_ME_LATER.md) — automatically maintained revisit queue for
  confusing wording, prerequisites, and shaky concepts.
- [Lessons learned](LESSONS_LEARNED.md) — study notes on shared Responsible AI practices and AI-500-specific evidence.
- [Certification training hub](../README.md) — GH-600-first sequence, shared Responsible AI practices, evidence labels, and cross-track mapping.
- [Official AI-500 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-500) — authoritative source for current exam objectives.

## Related tracks

- [GH-600 study companion](../gh-600/README.md), [readiness checklist](../gh-600/REPOSITORY_READINESS.md), and [lessons learned](../gh-600/LESSONS_LEARNED.md)
- [AI-103 study track](../ai-103/README.md), [readiness checklist](../ai-103/READINESS_CHECKLIST.md), and [lessons learned](../ai-103/LESSONS_LEARNED.md)

Shared Responsible AI principles apply here too, but this track needs AI-500-specific multi-agent implementation evidence. GitHub/Markdown design work is useful background and does not substitute for Microsoft Foundry or other platform-specific implementation evidence required by the current guide. The choice of a separate hands-on environment remains open under [Q-012](../../docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

Use AI-500 as a deep companion to GH-600 and AI-103 when multi-agent or
software-engineering ideas make the current topic clearer. It is not reserved
for the end of a fixed study sequence.
