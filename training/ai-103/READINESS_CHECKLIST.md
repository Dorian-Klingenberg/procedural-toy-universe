# AI-103 readiness checklist

- **Status:** Starter checklist; objective-by-objective mapping is not yet complete.
- **Exam focus:** Developing AI apps and agents on Azure.
- **Authoritative source:** [Official AI-103 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-103). Recheck the live guide before each study cycle.
- **Related study aid:** [AI-103 lessons learned](LESSONS_LEARNED.md).

## Preparation

- [ ] Review the current official study guide and record its update/review date.
- [ ] Map every current official objective to a learning resource, a practice activity, and evidence; keep the official guide as the source of truth.
- [ ] Identify which objectives require Azure or Python hands-on practice and which can first be explored through discussion or a mock exercise.
- [ ] Define a bounded Azure practice environment, identity and permission boundaries, data-handling rules, and spending limits before creating resources. See [Q-012](../../docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
- [ ] Use synthetic or otherwise approved data in prompts, examples, evaluation sets, logs, and traces.
- [ ] Complete and retain hands-on evidence for implementation objectives; record failures, troubleshooting, and what changed after evaluation.
- [ ] Apply the [shared Responsible AI checklist](../README.md#shared-responsible-ai-practice-checklist) and note the evidence that supports each relevant practice.
- [ ] Label each item of evidence as **hands-on**, **mock**, or **discussion/design**; do not count a proposal as an implementation.
- [ ] Record remaining gaps and choose one bounded next study task.
