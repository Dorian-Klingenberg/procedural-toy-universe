# Vision and design notes

## Experience
A playful, open-ended construction toolkit inspired by the flexibility of theme-park scenery editors, classic physical playsets, and interactive toy worlds. Players construct, share, explore, and modify locations and vehicles. Narratives and puzzles may emerge from coherent systems; authored missions are optional.

## Construction
- Grid-aligned structural pieces that interlock predictably.
- Named connector points independent of grids.
- Free placement for greebles, props, and irregular pieces.
- Any collection of pieces may be saved as a blueprint.
- Blueprints can expose a chosen subset of inherited connectors, or custom interfaces, to snap to other assemblies.
- Assemblies nest recursively, with local transforms and explicit interfaces.

## Progressive discovery
Unvisited areas need not be preselected. Opening a door commits a valid new space consistent with physical boundaries and everything previously observed. Seeds, generation rules, versioned module libraries, and committed discoveries support reproducibility and sharing. Multiple players may share one persistent reality; concurrency semantics remain undecided.

## Example, not product scope
A familiar saucer-shaped freighter illustrates a fixed exterior hull with configurable interiors, equipment, and room layouts. It is an example of constraints, not a request to reproduce copyrighted ship assets.

## Not initial scope
Destructibility, combat, planetary LOD, full ship simulation, network multiplayer, franchise-specific scenery, and a complete content library.
